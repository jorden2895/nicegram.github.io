# How does Nicegram AI work?

Nicegram AI adds assistant and content actions to eligible app surfaces. Depending
on the current build, these can include answering prompts, explaining a topic,
summarizing a chat, drafting or rewriting text, generating posts or images, helping
with a math problem, and invoking an AI shortcut from a conversation.

## Typical flow

1. Open the AI Assistant or an AI action in a supported chat or composer.
2. Review which conversation content will be included.
3. Enter a prompt or choose a predefined action.
4. Review the result before sending, publishing, or acting on it.

AI output can be wrong, incomplete, or fabricated. Verify financial, medical,
legal, security, and identity-related results independently.

## Availability and limits

The current official download page labels the built-in AI Assistant and AI Agent
Marketplace as **beta**. Store builds also describe AI features, so access can vary
by platform, version, subscription, usage balance, provider capacity, region, and
rollout.

Nicegram may change the model or provider behind an action. This Help Center does
not promise a specific model name unless the active product surface identifies it.

## Data sent for processing

Prompts and any context you deliberately include can be processed by Nicegram and a
selected model provider to return the result. Do not assume that opening an AI
action submits an entire chat, but always review the current screen and
[Privacy Policy](https://nicegram.me/privacy-policy) before sending sensitive text.
